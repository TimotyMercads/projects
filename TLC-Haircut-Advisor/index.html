                How the AI works

                In plain words, so you can explain it to anyone.

                Step by step

                1. Find the face. The app uses MediaPipe Face Landmarker, a free AI model from Google. It runs inside your browser and finds 478 points on your face (eyes, nose, lips, jawline, forehead) in every camera frame.
                2. Measure. From those points we measure the five things in the picture above.
                3. Average. When you tap Capture, we measure about 12 good frames and average them, so one blurry frame can't change the answer.
                4. Compare ratios. We compare the measurements as ratios. For example, a face much longer than it is wide is "Rectangle", and a forehead wider than the jaw is "Heart". The cut-off numbers are in one clearly labelled settings object in js/faceShape.js.
                5. Suggest. The face shape picks hairstyles and services from the lists in js/data.js. If the AI model is removed, there are no landmarks and the app can't work out a face shape at all.

                What can change the result

                • Dim or uneven light, glasses, and hair or a cap covering the forehead.
                • A turned or tilted head, or a camera held very close or at a strange angle.
                • The AI sees the upper forehead, not the real hairline, so the face length is an estimate.
                • Face-shape rules are simple and not exact. Treat the result as a starting point, and let your barber advise you in person.

                Your privacy

                • Everything runs on your own phone. No server, no account, no API key.
                • No photo or video is uploaded, saved, or recorded. The picture on the results page is erased when you leave it.
                • We collect no personal data, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
                • No generative AI is used. The app only measures; it doesn't create or edit pictures.
